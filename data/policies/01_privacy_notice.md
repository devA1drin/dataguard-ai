# Acme Retail PH — Customer Privacy Notice (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

> This document is a fictional sample written for the DataGuard AI demo project. "Acme Retail PH" is not a real company. It is not legal advice.

## What personal data we collect
We collect your full name, email address, mobile number, delivery address, order history, and loyalty points balance when you create an account or make a purchase. Payment card details are processed by our payment provider and we store only a masked card reference.

## Why we process your data
We process your personal data to fulfil orders, provide customer support, manage the loyalty program, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. Marketing emails, SMS promotions, personalized recommendations, analytics, and sharing with third-party partners happen only if you have given consent for that specific purpose.

## Legal basis
We aim to process personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the guidance of the National Privacy Commission. Processing for order fulfilment is based on our contract with you; processing for marketing is based on your consent.

## How long we keep your data
Account and order records are kept for as long as your account is active and for five years after your last transaction, unless a longer period is required by law. Marketing preferences are kept until you change them.

## Contact our Data Protection Officer
You may contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO) at dpo@acmeretail.example for any privacy question or complaint. You may also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission.
