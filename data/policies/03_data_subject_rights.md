# Acme Retail PH — Data Subject Rights Procedure (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

> Fictional sample document for a student demo project. Not legal advice.

## Your rights
Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, data subjects have rights that include the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correction (rectification), to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to file a complaint. This sample procedure covers how Acme Retail PH handles access, correction, and deletion requests.

## How to submit a request
Log in to the customer portal and open Data Rights Requests. Choose the request type — access, correction, or deletion — and describe what you need. You will receive a reference number and can track the status of your request: submitted, in review, completed, or rejected.

## Response time
We aim to respond to requests within 15 working days. If we need more time or more information to verify your identity, we will tell you through the portal.

## Deletion requests
When you ask us to delete your data, we delete or anonymize it unless we must keep certain records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons. If we cannot delete some data, we will explain why.

## Correction requests
If your name, address, contact details, or other information is wrong, you can request a correction. We will update our records and confirm once done.
