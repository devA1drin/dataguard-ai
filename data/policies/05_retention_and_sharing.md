# Acme Retail PH — Data Retention and Third-Party Sharing (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

> Fictional sample document for a student demo project. Not legal advice.

## Third-party sharing
We share personal data with delivery partners and payment providers only as needed to complete your order. We share data with marketing partners only if you have consented to third-party sharing. We do not sell customer data.

## Data processing agreements
Partners that process data on our behalf must sign a data processing agreement requiring them to protect personal data and to use it only for the agreed purpose.

## Retention schedule
Order and invoice records: five years after the transaction. Customer support tickets: two years after closure. Access logs: one year. Marketing preference records: until changed or the account is closed.

## Secure disposal
When the retention period ends, data is deleted from active systems and backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle. Paper records are shredded.
