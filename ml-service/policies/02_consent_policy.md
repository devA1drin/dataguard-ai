# Acme Retail PH — Consent Management Policy (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

> Fictional sample document for a student demo project. Not legal advice.

## Consent purposes
Customers can separately grant or withdraw consent for: marketing email, SMS promotions, personalized recommendations, third-party sharing, and analytics. Each purpose is optional and is not a condition for buying from us.

## Withdrawing consent
You can withdraw consent for any purpose — for example marketing emails or SMS promotions — at any time by switching it off on the My Consents page of the customer portal. Withdrawal takes effect immediately in our systems, and marketing campaigns are expected to stop within 48 hours. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before the withdrawal.

## Consent history
Every time you grant or withdraw consent, we record the purpose, the new status, the date and time, and the channel used. You can view your full consent history in the portal.

## Staff obligations
Employees must check the current consent status before using customer data for marketing. Exporting customer lists for campaigns without checking consent is a policy violation and must be reported to the DPO.
