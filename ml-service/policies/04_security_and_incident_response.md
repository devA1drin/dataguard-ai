# Acme Retail PH — Security and Incident Response Policy (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

> Fictional sample document for a student demo project. Not legal advice.

## Access control
Employees get access to customer data only when needed for their job (least privilege). Access to customer records is logged. Bulk exports of customer data require a documented business reason and manager approval.

## Monitoring
Access logs are monitored by an automated anomaly detection system. Alerts are flagged for human review by the security team and the Data Protection Officer. An alert is not proof of wrongdoing; every alert must be reviewed before any action is taken against an employee.

## Reporting incidents
Any employee who suspects a data breach, phishing attempt, lost device, misconfiguration, or policy violation must report it to the DPO and IT security immediately through the incident reporting tool.

## Breach notification
If a personal data breach is likely to cause serious harm to affected customers, the DPO evaluates whether notification to the National Privacy Commission and to affected data subjects is required, following the timelines set by applicable regulations.

## Lost or stolen devices
Company laptops and phones must use full-disk encryption and a screen lock. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours so IT can remotely wipe them.
